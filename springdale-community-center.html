University of Arkansas

Springdale Community Center

Spring 2023 · Public Works · Springdale, Arkansas · Professor Rachel Smith-Loerts

Springdale Community Center began with the design of a custom cast-concrete masonry module. The process started with full-scale 1:1 process models, which were refined into a new module at 1:2 scale. The system of modules was then developed as a collective assembly, tuned to modulate light as it passed through the wall, before a custom mold was built to cast the final modules in concrete at full scale.

The second stage combined the masonry system with a structural frame, designed to work with the existing module geometry and reinforce the same light effect established in the wall assembly. The final stage moved to the site: multiple massing orders were tested in 2D, two were developed in 3D against the site's topography, and the finalized plan and surrounding context were resolved — down to the building's floorplan, sections, and an oblique view of the completed site.
