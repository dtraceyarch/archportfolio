RED ⏹ BLOCK is a student-led speculative design studio focused on adaptive reuse, mixed-use development, and urban transformation. Operating through competitions, visual research, and conceptual projects, the studio explores how existing urban fabric can be reimagined through layered spatial systems, density, and evolving patterns of occupation.
