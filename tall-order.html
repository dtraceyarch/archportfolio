DANIEL TRACEY
University of Arkansas

Tall Order

Fall 2022 · Studio · University of Arkansas · Professor Laura Terry

Tall Order was the first major project of Daniel's undergraduate architecture degree, built around exploring modular design at architectural scale. The assignment required designing and constructing a tower from a single chosen module, with the final physical model standing at least five feet tall at 1/16" scale. Core design principles — color, pattern, and scale — were emphasized throughout, with creative freedom in material choice as long as the internal wooden core stayed intact.

The process began with a series of small models addressing three spatial conditions: sky, corner, and ground. These studies shaped the final concept, which was expected to carry all three conditions through unless a compelling design challenge required a documented and justified deviation. The chosen module was then scaled up and explored through iterative sketches and modeling, culminating in a twelve-foot-long working drawing — at least five feet of it devoted to the tower at final scale — presented alongside the constructed tower at final review.
