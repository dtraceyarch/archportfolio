Pergola Project

Summer 2021 · Residential · Weymouth, Massachusetts

While attending the Boston Architectural College's Summer Academy, the main project focused on designing a pergola for a local client in the Boston area. Our group was responsible for communicating directly with the client, understanding their needs and preferences, and developing a design that reflected their vision. The project concluded with a final presentation aimed at effectively pitching and selling the concept to the homeowner.

Overview. A direct-to-client design build early in Daniel's training — the project's value came as much from the client relationship as the design itself: translating a homeowner's needs into a buildable trellis and pergola, then pitching and defending that design in a final client presentation.
