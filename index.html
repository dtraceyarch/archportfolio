Daniel Tracey

Architectural Designer — Atlanta, GA

Architectural designer whose practice spans production housing, hospitality, education, commercial interiors, and new construction. Across five studios he has moved through every phase of a project — schematic design, design development, construction documents, site observation, quality control, RFI resolution, and the client-facing work of turning a design decision into something buildable.

Alongside project work, he builds custom AutoLISP and DCL tools that streamline AutoCAD documentation. His academic work has earned First Prize in Resin Architecture's national Rethink, Reimagine, Revitalize competition, Second Prize in Autodesk's international Make It Modular competition, and two Super Jury exhibitions. He holds a B.S. in Architectural Studies from the University of Arkansas with Highest Distinction and is continuing his education — and pursuing licensure — at Georgia Tech.
