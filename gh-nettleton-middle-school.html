G.H. Nettleton Middle School

Spring 2022 · Institutional · Frisco, Texas

G.H. Nettleton Middle School was the most significant and comprehensive undertaking of Daniel's high school architectural journey. Beginning in mid-September, the class was divided into three teams of four, each tasked with designing a new middle school for the city. Work on G.H. Nettleton was guided by bi-weekly critiques from two practicing architects at Corgan, who provided insight at every stage of the design process.

The project moved through four stages: site analysis and precedent research — including visits to existing local middle schools and interviews with staff — followed by concept sketches and early massing studies, then floorplan refinement and a full Revit model with site layout, fields, and access roads, and finally a compiled, professional-grade presentation to Corgan's local board of architects.
