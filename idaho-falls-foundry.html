Idaho Falls Foundry

Fall 2025 · Public Works & Commercial · Idaho Falls, Idaho
1st Place National Competition Competition Winners Page →

Idaho Falls Foundry is an adaptive reuse project that transforms a historic brick warehouse into a coworking and community hub in downtown Idaho Falls. The design preserves the building's industrial character while introducing flexible workspaces, shared offices, meeting rooms, and public amenities that respond to changing workforce needs and downtown activity.

Overview. First-place entry to Resin Architecture's RETHINK, REIMAGINE, REVITALIZE competition. The program reflects regional growth in professional services, education, healthcare administration, and technology-related fields, many of which increasingly rely on hybrid and independent work models. Private offices and open workstations are arranged to maximize daylight and flexibility, while support spaces and public functions are positioned to allow events without disrupting daily operations. A café near the main circulation area encourages informal meetings and draws in pedestrians, hotel guests, and nearby visitors. Outdoor improvements — including a visible front entry and a small amphitheater for talks and presentations — extend activity into evenings and weekends. Minimal structural changes, upgraded windows, and added skylights improve performance while respecting the historic masonry.
