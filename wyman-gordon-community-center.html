Wyman-Gordon Community Center

Spring 2022 · Institutional & Public Works · Dixmoor, Illinois

This project reimagines an abandoned powerplant in Dixmoor, Illinois as a vibrant community hub. Once part of the village's industrial backbone, the building now stands vacant and overgrown — contributing to local decline. Through adaptive reuse, the structure transforms into a multi-use facility that restores dignity to the site and provides long-needed public amenities.

The renovated building offers flexible space for a range of community functions: a recreation center, town meeting hall, library annex, and classroom space for after-school and adult learning programs. Public green space and improved landscaping create a more welcoming, walkable environment while connecting residents of all ages and backgrounds. By preserving the industrial character of the structure and layering in new functions, the project both honors Dixmoor's past and supports its future — offering a central place for gathering, learning, and growing together.

The project reads adaptive reuse as a civic act — turning a symbol of local industrial decline into a shared amenity, with rooms scaled for very different uses (gym, library, auditorium, childcare, classrooms) all held within the original industrial shell to keep the building's original character legible.
