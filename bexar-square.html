Bexar Square

Summer 2022 · Residential & Public Works · Dallas, Texas
2nd Prize Competition Entry Page →

Bexar Square is an affordable housing project in Dallas that addresses homelessness by repurposing shipping containers into modular townhomes. Developed for Autodesk's Make It Modular 2022 contest, the project responds to a challenge to create structures using modular, adaptable, and circular economy-focused approaches.

The development includes 101 townhomes constructed primarily from 84 shipping containers each, providing durable, weather-resistant, and cost-effective shelter for multiple families near transit, parks, and schools. Bexar Square prioritizes "design for deconstruction," allowing units to be refurbished, reconfigured, or repurposed as community needs evolve — reducing waste and enabling rapid, affordable construction. Rent follows the 30% income rule to keep housing accessible to low-income families. The project also incorporates community-building elements like the Farmhouse, a food service center offering affordable meals and jobs to residents, and Bexar Plaza, an outdoor gathering space for education, recreation, and social events.

Overview. Zoning and housing codes support the project's mission, with mixed-income housing development bonuses facilitating affordable unit allocation. Looking ahead, Bexar Square's flexible design enables a transition to mixed-use spaces with shops and offices, generating employment and economic growth. By embracing modular construction, circular economy principles, and community-focused design, Bexar Square demonstrates how architecture can create sustainable, adaptable, and affordable housing solutions that serve both present and future needs.
